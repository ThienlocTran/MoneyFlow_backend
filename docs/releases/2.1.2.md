## Required Local Env

```env
MONEYFLOW_ASR_PROVIDER=azure_speech
AZURE_SPEECH_KEY=<your-azure-speech-key>
AZURE_SPEECH_REGION=japaneast
AZURE_SPEECH_LANGUAGE=vi-VN
MONEYFLOW_ASR_MAX_AUDIO_SECONDS=15
MONEYFLOW_ASR_TIMEOUT_SECONDS=30
```

Never commit real Azure or Cloudinary secrets.

## P10E Domain Rule

- Income may be saved without wallet.
- Income with a wallet affects the selected wallet balance under current ledger rules.
- Income without wallet saves with `affectsWalletBalance=false`, appears in transactions/statistics, and does not change wallet balance.
- Expense, transfer, savings, and debt wallet rules are unchanged.
- MoneyFlow does not force, guess, or auto-select a wallet for income.

## Known Limitations

- If Azure omits a number entirely, MoneyFlow warns instead of inventing amount.
- If Cloudinary/storage is not configured, transcript can save but audio playback cannot be guaranteed.
- Voice V2 Q&A is not part of 2.1.2.
- Receipt OCR is not part of 2.1.2.
